# Style Bible & Continuity Lock

## Master prompt (tempel di awal SETIAP gambar)
PHOTOREAL cinematic still from a premium Hollywood psychological thriller, shot on 35mm film (ARRI Alexa look, anamorphic-free spherical lens), 16:9, 3840x2160 (min 1920x1080). Deep navy blue shadows, desaturated charcoal blacks, cold blue ambient moonlight, very subtle warm practical lights, controlled contrast, high dynamic range, physically accurate lighting, subtle atmospheric haze, subtle film grain, natural cinematic shadows, professional production design, realistic modern Indonesian urban apartment architecture. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no random clutter.

## Negative prompt
people, faces, text, letters, logos, watermark, cartoon, illustration, CGI look, oversaturated, warm orange cast, fisheye distortion, clutter

## Continuity lock (wajib)
1. Generate **apartment.jpg dulu**. Jadikan gambar acuan (image reference / style reference / seed yang sama) untuk semua gambar lain.
2. Tata ruang apartemen TETAP: jendela besar hujan di **kiri**, sofa charcoal di tengah, TV di dinding kiri-belakang, meja kecil di kanan sofa, pintu masuk di **kanan**, cahaya bulan biru dari kiri.
3. Pintu di hallway = pintu yang sama dengan `props/door` (kayu gelap, lubang intip di tengah atas, gagang logam tuas, rasio 1:2,3).
4. Ponsel, jam digital, TV: satu desain, tiap kali dibuat ulang gunakan gambar prop sebelumnya sebagai referensi.
5. Color grade seragam. Kalau ada gambar yang menyimpang, regenerate — jangan dikoreksi manual.

## Bahasa kamera
35 mm (establishing/environment) · 50 mm (close-up karakter) · 85 mm (close-up suspense, DOF tipis) · dolly lambat · handheld terkendali.
Komposisi environment: sisakan ruang kosong di depan/atas untuk parallax dan pergerakan kamera; objek penting tidak menempel tepi frame.
